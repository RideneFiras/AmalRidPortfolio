Amal Ridene – Portfolio Content Research
Profile Overview
Amal Ridene is a Tunisian sustainable development professional and youth leader active in  climate
finance ,  youth empowerment , and  stakeholder engagement . She is currently the Curator of the
World  Economic  Forum’s  Global  Shapers  Tunis  Hub  and  a  Chevening  Scholar  pursuing  an  MSc  in
Sustainable Development at the University of Sussex (Science Policy Research Unit) . Amal previously
worked as an Investor Engagement Officer  at AfricInvest (a leading pan-African private equity firm),
where  she  supported  fundraising,  due  diligence,  investor  relations,  communications,  events,  and
climate  initiatives .  In  parallel,  she  has  served  as  one  of  Tunisia’s  Young  Climate  Change
Negotiators , acting as a party delegate at the UN Climate Change Conferences (COP26, COP27, COP28)
and leading her country’s Climate Finance  negotiation team . Amal also holds or has held multiple
leadership and advisory roles – for instance, she is a former board member of the World Organization
of the Scout Movement (WOSM) and currently chairs the 2024 World Scout Conference . She sits on
the  boards  or  advisory  panels  of  initiatives  like  RIVET ,  the  Arab  Youth4COP  Initiative ,  UNICEF’s
Generation Unlimited  (Young People’s Action Team), and the Glasgow Financial Alliance for Net Zero
(GFANZ) . This rich mix of experiences underpins Amal’s multidisciplinary profile across the three
domains of climate finance, youth empowerment, and stakeholder engagement.

to the United Nations. She exemplifies how investing in young people’s skills, leadership, and networks
can create a powerful ripple effect for sustainable development .
