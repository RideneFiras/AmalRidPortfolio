Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working together

What types of speaking engagements do you accept?

I'm available for conferences, corporate events, university talks, and virtual presentations. I particularly enjoy panels and workshops where I can engage directly with the audience. Topics I speak on include climate finance, youth leadership, sustainable development, and stakeholder engagement.

Do you offer mentorship or advisory services?

Yes! I'm passionate about supporting young professionals and organizations working on climate and sustainability issues. I offer both formal advisory positions and informal mentorship relationships, depending on the scope and time commitment required.

What's your availability for international travel?

As a Chevening Scholar currently based in the UK, I have some flexibility for international engagements. I'm also very comfortable with virtual presentations and hybrid events. Let's discuss your event details and we can find a format that works.

How can we collaborate on research or publications?

I'm always interested in contributing to meaningful research, particularly on blended finance, youth engagement in climate policy, and stakeholder collaboration models. I can contribute as a co-author, provide expert insights, or share case study experiences.

What's the best way to reach you for urgent inquiries?

For time-sensitive matters, LinkedIn messaging tends to be the fastest way to reach me. For detailed proposals or collaboration discussions, the contact form above works well as it allows you to provide all necessary context upfront.
